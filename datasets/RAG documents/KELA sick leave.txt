Kela (the Social Insurance Institution of Finland) provides sickness allowance to compensate for loss of income if you are incapable of working due to illness. The allowance is generally available for periods of incapacity lasting less than one year. 
Here are the Kela instructions for sick leave and sickness allowance:
1. Act Immediately When You Fall Ill 
Notify your employer: Inform your employer immediately when your sick leave starts.
See a doctor: To qualify for sickness allowance, you need a medical certificate (Form A) from a doctor stating you are incapable of work.
Waiting period: Kela pays sickness allowance only after a waiting period, which usually consists of the first day of illness + the following 9 working days. During this time, you may receive sick pay from your employer based on your employment contract. 
2. Apply for Sickness Allowance 
When to apply: Apply for the allowance within 2 months of the date you became unable to work.
How to apply: Use the OmaKela e-service (available in Finnish and Swedish) to fill in the application, or use form SV 8e (PDF) and send it by mail.
Documentation: Ensure your doctor sends the medical certificate directly to Kela or attach it to your application. 
3. Prolonged Illness
After 60 days: If your illness lasts more than 60 working days, you will need a more detailed medical statement (Form B) to continue receiving the allowance. Kela will review your need for rehabilitation.
After 90 days: If you are employed, you need a statement from an occupational health doctor regarding your ability to continue working by the 90-day mark.
Maximum duration: Sickness allowance is paid for a maximum of 300 working days (about one year). 
4. Partial Sickness Allowance
If you are able to return to work on a part-time basis (40–60% of full-time hours), you can apply for partial sickness allowance to help you stay employed and return to full-time work. 
5. Special Situations
Unemployed: If you are unemployed, you should still apply for sickness allowance from Kela if you are too sick to work.
Students: If you cannot study due to illness, you may be eligible for sickness allowance. Note that you cannot receive student financial aid and sickness allowance at the same time.
Self-employed: If you have YEL/MYEL insurance, your allowance is based on that income. If you do not have insurance, you may still qualify.
