Behaving on social media requires treating online interactions with the same respect, empathy, and professionalism as in-person situations. Key practices include verifying information before sharing, protecting personal privacy by limiting oversharing, using polite language, and avoiding heated arguments or posting inappropriate content. 
Essential Social Media Etiquette Guidelines
Think Before Posting: Consider if you would be comfortable with your boss or family seeing your post. Utilize a "24-hour rule" for sensitive content to ensure it is appropriate.
Be Respectful and Empathetic: Kindness is paramount, even when disagreeing with others. Avoid trolling, petty comments, or bullying.
Protect Privacy and Safety: Avoid posting sensitive information like home addresses, phone numbers, or real-time locations to avoid safety risks.
Verify Information: Do not spread misinformation or fake news.
Give Credit: Always cite the original creator when sharing content.
Professionalism is Key: Maintain a positive tone and avoid posting complaints about work, colleagues, or competitors. Use proper grammar and spelling.
Manage Connections: Only connect with people you know and trust.
Respect Boundaries: Do not tag or share photos of others without their permission.
Handle Negativity Wisely: If you encounter harassment, report and block the user rather than engaging further.
Moderate Sharing: Avoid overwhelming feeds with too many posts. 
Following these guidelines helps build a positive digital footprint and fosters a less toxic online environment.
